Who is the user?

You are an international student, or a staff member who advises them. You want plain-English answers to visa questions, like working on campus or applying for OPT, that cite the exact federal regulation.

Where should you go?

Application: http://149.165.147.165:8000

README: https://github.com/RAkupper/Assignment_for_6700#readme

API docs: http://149.165.147.165:8000/docs

How should you get started?

Open the app, choose a visa type, and type a question.

Leave "Rules as of" blank for today's rules, or pick a past date. Click a numbered citation to see the regulation text behind the answer. Try "Can I work on campus?", a question the rules do not cover, and one question with different dates. Check that each answer matches the passages it cites.

What can the skeleton do, and what can it not do?

It can answer questions about 8 CFR 214 (nonimmigrant classes) from eCFR, filter by visa type and date, show its sources, and say when the rules do not cover a question. It cannot yet search the Federal Register, USCIS Policy Manual, or SEVP guidance. It runs a small language model, so answers can be wrong, miss a citation, or take several seconds. It is a research prototype, not legal advice.
